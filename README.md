# Community Beacon App
Two-way communication app for local assistance in disaster situations. Text and call based to increase inclusivity and range in low wifi and signal areas.

### How It Works
Utilizes a map that collects and displays user-generated tickets based on various emergency classifications:
  - Medical
  - Hazards/Obstructions
  - Food/Water
  - Transportation
  - Other

Users select their relevant emergency, provide details on location and images if relevant to provide responders with an accurate idea of where to head. A pin appears on the map in the color relevant to the level of emergency. Community volunteers may respond to these points on the map, and may select pending to indicate an attempt of help on the way. Once assistance is provided, the user can confirm the status as resolved.

### Additional Features
There is a page that displays instructions for what to do in an emergency to provide a baseline of assistance to users.
